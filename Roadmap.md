# InkdFrames Roadmap 🎞️

## 🎯 Current Goal: V1.0

The goal of V1 is simple:

> Create a usable animation app where an animator can draw, manage frames,
> preview an animation, save their work, reopen it, and export the result.

Anything that isn't essential to that goal goes into the Post-V1 Idea Vault.
